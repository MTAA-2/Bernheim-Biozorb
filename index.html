The BioZorb Lawsuit

Our law firm is filing claims on behalf of patients who suffered complications after implantation of the BioZorb™ breast tissue marker, manufactured by Hologic, Inc.

The lawsuits allege that some patients experienced serious complications following implantation, including pain, infection, device migration, erosion, delayed healing, inflammation, and other adverse events that required additional medical treatment or corrective surgery.

If you experienced complications after receiving a BioZorb implant, you may have legal rights. Contact Us today for a free, confidential case evaluation. There is no upfront cost, and no fee unless we win compensation for you.

Do You Qualify?

Alleged Complications

The lawsuits allege complications including the following — and claims are being evaluated where complications led to further treatment or surgery:

Pain

Ongoing or severe pain after implantation of the tissue marker.

Infection

Infection at or around the implant site following surgery.

Device Migration

The BioZorb device moving from where it was implanted.

Erosion

The device eroding through tissue or skin after implantation.

Delayed Healing

The surgical site failing to heal properly or on schedule.

Inflammation

Inflammation and other adverse events after implantation.

Revision Surgery

Complications that resulted in additional corrective surgery.

Device Removal

Complications that required the BioZorb device to be removed.

Other Significant Treatment

Other significant medical treatment made necessary by complications.
